# Imperial International School
## A bilingual landing page for an evolving school website

**Shahad Fuad Kasib · Web development and technology leadership**  
**Focus:** React, TypeScript, Arabic–English localization, responsive interface design

> This case study covers the earlier bilingual landing page. The wider school website and portal roadmap are separate work.

### Context

The school needed an online presence that could communicate in Arabic and English while its wider website was being developed. The inspected version delivers a branded “coming soon” landing page with language switching and contact links.

### My contribution

I worked on the school’s bilingual website architecture and later took ownership of its website and portal roadmap as CTO. This case study covers the earlier landing-page implementation.

### What the code demonstrates

- **Language selection:** The initial language is selected from a valid URL language parameter, a stored preference, or the browser’s Arabic language setting, with English as the fallback.
- **Persistent preferences:** Language selection is saved locally for later visits.
- **Direction-aware presentation:** Switching language updates both the document language and its reading direction.
- **Separate language content:** English and Arabic copy are maintained as separate dictionaries.
- **Responsive styling:** Flexible widths, responsive typography, and a mobile breakpoint adapt the page layout.
- **Clear contact paths:** The interface provides messaging, telephone, and email links.

### Product decisions

The page gives visitors a focused experience: identify the school, understand that a new website is being developed, choose a language, and find a contact route. The implementation keeps the interaction small while supporting two reading directions.

### Scope and evidence

This version implements a landing page. Completed student, parent, and staff portals are outside the scope of this case study.

No accessibility score, search ranking improvement, conversion rate, or performance benchmark is claimed. The source was reviewed, but the site was not run or tested during this review.

### Portfolio value

This is a supporting frontend project that demonstrates localization and responsive implementation. SHIP Hub is the stronger flagship for full-stack engineering and operational workflow design.
